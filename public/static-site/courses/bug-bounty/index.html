Course overview

Bug Bounty Fundamentals turns scattered tips into a repeatable workflow. Learn how experienced researchers choose targets, map attack surface, validate findings and communicate impact.

The sessions stay grounded in legal, responsible testing. You will practise on safe targets and learn the judgement that keeps research useful and respectful.
