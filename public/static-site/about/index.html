About the academy

Learn the work.
Not the hype.

A small, independent cybersecurity academy built around live teaching, practical questions and honest guidance.

Naitik — instructor

Teaching from the
real-world side.

I'm a security researcher who spends most of the week testing web applications and APIs, and the rest of it teaching people how to do the same thing properly.

I don't record lectures and sell them. Every class I run is live, because the part that actually makes someone good at security is the messy part — the question you ask mid-exercise and the target that behaves differently than the slide said it would.

Teaching philosophy

Show the why

02

03

04
