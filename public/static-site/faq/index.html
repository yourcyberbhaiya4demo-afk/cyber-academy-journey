Live training

Are the classes completely live?
Yes. Every session is conducted live by the instructor at the scheduled time. There is no pre-recorded lecture library.
Are sessions recorded?
The course is designed around attending live. Recordings are not part of the product, so plan to join the scheduled sessions.
What happens if only one student enrolls?
The class still runs live. There is no minimum-student requirement and a batch is never cancelled for low enrollment.
How long is each class?
Each live session runs about 90 minutes, including practical work and questions.
Do I need previous cybersecurity experience?
No advanced experience is required. Basic computer and web familiarity is enough for the beginner-friendly courses.
What equipment do I need?
A laptop or desktop, a stable internet connection, and the willingness to practise alongside the session.

Enrollment & payment

How do I enroll?
Click ENROLL NOW on the course page, enter your details, review the course fee, and complete the payment step.
How does payment work?
You pay the exact course fee via UPI or bank transfer, then submit your payment proof through the enrollment form.
How long does payment verification take?
Verification normally takes up to 24 hours. It is a manual check — nothing is verified automatically.
How will I receive class details?
Once your payment is verified, class details are shared using the email and WhatsApp number you provided.
